# Legacy parameter row classification and repair semantics

Status: research decision for [Issue #670](https://github.com/tzrea1-Q/WiseEff/issues/670), against `origin/main@e895bedefa90c2d00c0dcc9e1f6e7496c060534d`.

## Decision

Legacy parameter rows must be classified by their complete relational graph, not by a property name. In particular, the observed `iin_max` and `init_para` pairs are not proven duplicates: in the current model, each subjectless platform DTS draft is retained as ambiguous staging evidence, while each organization-owned manual NodeType draft is a separate governance proposal tied to an observed node type. The absence of a DriverSchema root for `vendor/huawei,charging_core` and `vendor/huawei,wireless_sc`, together with the presence of only `nodetype:charging_core` and `nodetype:wireless_sc` subjects, leaves no driver identity to merge into, promote, or delete against. Property-key equality cannot fill that gap. This follows the catalog ADR's identity and readiness rules and the populated-upgrade migration's explicit prohibition on property-key matching, history deletion, and category guessing ([ADR-0039](../adr/0039-effective-driver-parameter-catalog.md#L21-L70), [migration 0127](../../server/migrations/0127_repair_populated_effective_driver_catalog.sql#L1-L14)).

Classification describes what a row means in the **current legacy model**; it does not endorse that model as the destination. [Wayfinder #668](https://github.com/tzrea1-Q/WiseEff/issues/668) fixes the replacement boundary: the Platform schema catalog is the sole structural truth, organizations own only registration and placement, and proposals, observations, values, and history live outside current definitions. Consequently, an organization definition or overlay cannot survive the target cutover merely because the current effective-definition selector recognizes it.

## Scope and evidence boundary

This note classifies a **definition bundle**: one `parameter_specs` row plus any `driver_schemas` root or `dts_property_specs` property row, attribution subject and subtype row, revisions, placements, bindings, observations, and governance records that refer to it. The classes below are ordered and mutually exclusive: apply the first matching class, R0 through R10.

The affected-server evidence supplied for this research, and not independently re-queried in this repository worktree, is:

- before repair, 59 organization manual draft NodeType rows;
- before repair, 78 platform subjectless active DTS rows: 59 unlinked property surfaces and 19 driver roots;
- 23 canonical active driver properties and 33 active NodeType properties;
- after repair, `iin_max` and `init_para` each remained as one platform DTS draft with null subject, null `driver_schema`, and a draft revision, plus one organization manual draft NodeType row with an observed node-type module;
- DriverSchema-root queries for `vendor/huawei,charging_core` and `vendor/huawei,wireless_sc` returned zero; only `nodetype:charging_core` and `nodetype:wireless_sc` subjects existed; and
- reconciliation dry-run returned zero candidates because platform and NodeType rows were outside its scope.

The last observation is consistent with the executable reconciler: it selects only organization-owned rows and explicitly excludes `node-type-definition` subjects ([reconciler scope](../../server/modules/parameter-specs/definitionReconciliation.ts#L302-L310), [candidate exclusions](../../server/modules/parameter-specs/definitionReconciliation.ts#L426-L509)). A zero-candidate dry-run is therefore **not** evidence that platform staging rows or NodeType proposals are canonical, safe to delete, or otherwise clean.

## Strong and weak evidence rules

Automatic identity repair requires a conjunction of strong evidence; no individual item is sufficient:

- exact owner alignment across the spec, subject, subtype, DriverSchema/root, placement, module, and binding;
- the expected subject kind and its concrete child row;
- equality between `parameter_specs.property_key` and `dts_property_specs.property_key`;
- one active definition revision, an active schema revision, and a unique valid placement;
- for reconciliation, a current logical-node `compatible` that uniquely selects one active platform schema, plus exact value shape, units, current values, and a collision-free destination; and
- for module cutover, an `origin = 'auto'` module with the exact source key, a complete platform schema, uniqueness, and no conflicting placement.

These conjunctions are encoded in the reconciliation classification and apply-time recheck, and in migration 0128's intentionally narrow auto-module cutover ([reconciler classification](../../server/modules/parameter-specs/definitionReconciliation.ts#L646-L950), [reconciler apply recheck](../../server/modules/parameter-specs/definitionReconciliation.ts#L1093-L1451), [migration 0128](../../server/migrations/0128_repair_driver_placement_subject_cutover.sql#L1-L155)).

The following are weak evidence only and must never establish identity by themselves:

- equal property keys, display names, source-namespace suffixes, specification-key tokens, or source kinds;
- a node label, NodeType subject, or observed node-type module presented as evidence of DriverRegistration;
- a binding or occurrence without the exact current config revision and compatible match;
- raw active-row counts; or
- a reconciliation dry-run that did not have those rows in scope.

The surrogate identity migration is explicit that a null-subject legacy row remains outside the canonical `(organization_id, attribution_subject_id, property_key)` identity, and ADR-0039 rejects name-based inference ([migration 0090](../../server/migrations/0090_parameter_spec_identity_surrogate.sql#L1-L13), [migration 0090 uniqueness](../../server/migrations/0090_parameter_spec_identity_surrogate.sql#L86-L142), [ADR-0039](../adr/0039-effective-driver-parameter-catalog.md#L46-L70)).

## Hard unknowns for the rehearsal fixture

[Issue #671](https://github.com/tzrea1-Q/WiseEff/issues/671) must capture enough redacted relational shape to answer all of these before any follow-up migration changes the residual rows:

1. The exact schema/migration set, deterministic checksums, and pre/post row counts for R0-R10, including whether any apparent member actually fails an owner, revision, key, subtype, or placement invariant.
2. For every one of the 59 surfaces and 59 organization proposals, the full one-to-many relation to subjects, subtype rows, modules and their `origin`, current binding tips and config revisions, occurrences, review tasks, occurrence decisions, matcher overrides, audit rows, and prior reconciliation items. A same-key pair is not a relation.
3. Whether the latest applicable logical-node revisions contain authoritative `compatible` values for `charging_core` or `wireless_sc`, and the exact DTS source-span/overlay-base provenance. Node labels alone cannot answer this.
4. Whether an authoritative vendor DriverSchema/DriverRegistration can be imported or identified, and whether exactly one active platform candidate matches compatible, property key, value shape, units, constraints, and current values.
5. Whether auto or curated modules, duplicate placements, business categories, or destination-binding collisions exist across affected organizations. Curated or ambiguous destinations must remain blocked.
6. Whether any candidate deletion has zero protected references and satisfies audit retention. The current evidence does not show that either the platform drafts or the organization proposals are disposable.
7. Rehearsed expectations for the clean, pre-repair, and post-repair shapes: idempotence, stable-id mappings, no row-count drift outside the declared class, one Platform-only current-definition projection, separate proposal/observation/history reads, verifier findings, and reconcile dry-run/apply results on real PostgreSQL.

Until that fixture exists, the safe **legacy-model repair** boundary is unchanged: apply only the existing unanimous-root backfill, subjectless-surface demotion, deterministic placement, and exact auto-module/current-binding cutovers; preserve all other evidence and block. That boundary is not the target model. The replacement cutover must materialize only authoritative Platform Driver/NodeType definitions and their current revisions, convert organization usage to registration/placement, map external legacy IDs, and move R6/R7/R8 material to review proposal, observation, policy, or archive aggregates outside current-definition reads. Unknown or ambiguous evidence remains non-definition evidence. The completed populated-upgrade plan requires evidence-preserving, idempotent repair rather than identity guesses, while Wayfinder #668 explicitly retires organization schema overlays and definition overrides ([completed plan](../exec-plans/completed/2026-08-29-effective-driver-populated-upgrade-repair.md#L20-L91), [Issue #649](https://github.com/tzrea1-Q/WiseEff/issues/649), [Wayfinder #668](https://github.com/tzrea1-Q/WiseEff/issues/668)).
